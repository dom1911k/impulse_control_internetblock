# Update: dashboard, tidier window, play plans that stay

What's new:
- **Home** tab with your stats: WoW today / this week / this month / all time (with your limits),
  a 30-day chart (light = WoW profile active, dark = the game actually running), average per day,
  sessions, days without an unlock request, unlock and commitment counts, time per profile.
- **Five tabs:** Home, Play time, Programs, History, Settings. The output box at the bottom is gone –
  results appear as a short message under the banner; long texts open in their own window.
- **Profile cards** have one button. Test lock is in Settings → Practice & test, Preview is in
  Programs → *Preview firewall changes*. Rarely needed things are under Settings → Advanced.
- **Play plans are kept:** with normal internet every change in Play time saves by itself
  ("✓ Saved"). During a lock you get *Save changes* and an "unsaved changes" warning; switching tabs
  no longer throws edits away. Setup → *Search again* keeps your plans, own profiles and added programs.
