NetGuard – before you install

NetGuard blocks the internet for everything except the programs the active profile allows
(for example World of Warcraft, Battle.net and Spotify). Steam is always blocked.

How it holds:
- A background service (running as SYSTEM) owns the firewall. It repairs any change within
  seconds and keeps your profile across reboots.
- While a lock is active, NetGuard also protects itself from administrators: it can't simply
  be stopped, edited or uninstalled.
- Stricter is always one click. Looser needs an unlock request: a reason, a 45-minute wait,
  then retyping a passage – at most twice a day.

Your way out in an emergency:
- The setup wizard creates a recovery code. Write it on paper and keep it somewhere
  inconvenient. It ends any lock immediately (Start menu: "NetGuard Emergency Restore").
- Uninstalling (from inside NetGuard: Settings -> Advanced -> Uninstall NetGuard) always leaves the PC with
  normal internet.

Nothing is locked right after installing. You decide when to start.
